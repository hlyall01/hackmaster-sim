# Barbarian Requirements and Restrictions

## Requirements
- Alignment cannot be Lawful.
- Strength 14+.
- Constitution 14+.
- Average Survival.
- Must purchase first barbarian talent within character creation.

## Special Restrictions
### Magic Phobia
Barbarians are superstitious and fear magic (a cultural taboo). They must make a morale
check at -2 level to morale the first time they encounter any fantastic creature or
magic (friendly or otherwise). Failure means they run away to safety and hide cowering.

If they witness the vulnerability of the creature (view its remains or see companions
survive to tell of its destruction), they gain confidence. Encountering the specific
creature a second time requires only a morale check at -1 level. Observing two instances
of the beast slain by mortals removes any doubt, and no morale check is required.

All barbarians distrust mages, even those they know well. They presume friendly behavior
is a ruse to gain their trust and will never allow a mage to cast magic on them (even
for beneficial purposes). They seek vengeance if they become aware of surreptitious
dweomers being laid upon them.

If a barbarian ever becomes able to use magic, they are no longer afraid of mages and
magic, but still retain their phobia of creatures with unnatural powers.
