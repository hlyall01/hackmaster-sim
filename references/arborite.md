
# Arborite race and racial talents

Arborites (also called arborians) have 5 base HP, Medium size, and count as Small for knockback. Creation adjustments are STR −2, INT +1, WIS −2, DEX +4, CON −4, CHA +2 and LKS +2. They speak Arborian natively. The full appearance, free skill purchases, innate talents and iron-contact restriction are retained in the race details.

Innate combat benefits are +2 Defense and an initiative die one step better (d10 instead of d12). In natural surroundings they gain −1 Speed, −2 Initiative and +1 natural DR. Charm and illusion resistance use the existing resistance rules. Hiding and Sneaking receive +50 in natural surroundings; these bonuses and the free skill purchases are recorded as race rules because the simulator does not resolve ordinary skill checks.

The Iron weapon checkbox is independent of material tier. Primary and offhand flags are separate. For a ranged weapon the flag refers to the striking projectile. An iron body hit against an Arborite rolls twice the damage dice, retaining flat weapon, Strength, mastery and material modifiers once. Arborhide replaces double with triple. Mounted dice and critical/opening dice use the same multiplier. Shield-hit damage remains damage to the shield rather than direct iron contact with the Arborite.

- **Barkskin (5 RP):** natural-surroundings racial DR becomes +3 total, replacing +1.
- **Shillelagh (5 RP):** sticks, clubs and staves use all bonuses of the melee mastery group with the highest total ATK + DMG + SPD reduction + DEF. Ranged and shield groups are excluded. Ties are deterministic. Recorded mastery values remain unchanged; shield mastery still governs shield defense and limits shielded attack speed normally.
- **Silent Defiance (5 RP):** optionally hold still instead of acting under any mental compulsion, magical or otherwise. Set the mental-compulsion condition and the choice in Core. Holding still prevents attacks, movement and spellcasting, without granting immunity to the mental effect. The combat API can change this condition while a fight is running.
- **Arborhide (10 RP, requires Barkskin):** racial DR becomes +8 everywhere, replacing the lesser racial bonuses. Iron damage dice are tripled rather than doubled. The iron phobia is retained in the description and conditions panel; no unspecified phobia rolls or contact-burn amounts are invented.
- **Overcome Naivety (10 RP, requires age over 500):** +3 derived Wisdom. Age 500 is ineligible; age 501 is eligible. Saved base Wisdom is not mutated, preventing repeated bonuses on reload.
- **Warrior Woad (10 RP, requires Average Artistry [painting]):** mark painting eligibility and warpaint applied before combat, after its 10-minute preparation. The first positive hacking/piercing body wound consumes that preparation and activates d6 additional Strength points and +6 Defense for 5d12p seconds. The Strength bonus is resolved through the ability table and applies to either weapon and unarmed damage; defense applies once to either ranged-defense choice. Further wounds do not reroll or extend the same application. Both Physical and Mental saves are rolled independently against d20p+11 at expiration, using level plus the appropriate save bonus. Either failure causes about one hour (3,600 seconds) of unconsciousness. Woad's existing Armeroci availability is preserved.

Racial conditions, iron flags and talent selections survive fighter presets and online character documents. Timed effects, rolled bonuses, expiry saves and unconsciousness are per-fight state and reset for a new fight. Age Gracefully, aging, contact burns, phobias and ordinary skill checks remain campaign rules rather than newly invented simulation mechanics.
