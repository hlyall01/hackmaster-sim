# Knight Requirements and Restrictions

## Requirements
- Alignment must be Lawful.
- Strength 12+.
- Wisdom 10+.
- Constitution 12+.
- Charisma 12+.
- Lance proficiency.
- Advanced Riding.
- Average Diplomacy.
- Average Persuasion.
- Average Recruiting.
- Average or higher Honor.
- Must be knighted.

## Special Restrictions
- A knight may not employ ranged weapons against an honorable foe, nor polearms or other reach weapons.
  Against ignoble or lesser opponents, bows, crossbows, polearms, and siege weapons are allowed.
- A knight may never purposely attack an intelligent foe from the rear (vermin and beasts are exempt).
- Breaking these restrictions immediately costs 1 point of honor.
