**Wingless (0 RP)**
_must be chosen within character creation, mutually exclusive with winged_
Unfettered by wings, your dexterity is improved by 2.

**Winged (0 RP)**
_must be chosen within character creation, mutually exclusive with wingless_
You may use your large bat-like wings to glide. You may glide 1 foot down for every 2 feet of forward movement in one second, or dive 1 foot for every 1 foot traveled forward. You may also turn while aerial and diving in the same manner as horses, with the same facing restrictions and turning diameters (no ride check necessary).

**Glide** | 2½ across, 1¼ down
| 5 across, 2½ down | 7½ across, 3¾ down | 10 across, 5 down |
**Dive** | 2½ across, 2½ down | 5 across, 5 down | 7½ across, 7½ down | 10 across, 10 down |
**Aerial Mobility (5 RP)**
_requires Strengthened Wings_
You double the degrees you can turn while flying. In addition, you may climb straight up while hovering at half speed. Lastly, you can now dive down at full speed without needing forward momentum, although you will still take fall damage if you dive straight into the ground.

**Terrifying (5 RP)**
You gain a +3 on mastery die rolls for Intimidate, in addition to a free Intimidate purchase at each level. These skill purchases are retroactive. In addition, use your Strength score as if it is your Charisma score to determine Morale.

**Echolocation (5 RP)**
_requires Advanced Listening_
You may detect objects in your environment by sensing echoes from those objects by actively creating sounds – for example, by lightly stomping your foot or making clicking noises. You can accurately determine the location and size of objects within 30 ft. by interpreting the sound waves reflected off of them. This will not allow you to accurately locate things around corners or behind walls.

**Nightstalker (5 RP)**
_requires Echolocation_
You gain a +50 bonus to hiding and sneaking while within complete darkness.

**Light Adaptation (5 RP)**
Your eyes have adapted to sunlight, and other bright lights. You no longer receive a penalty to attack in bright light.

**Leaper (10 RP)**
Your jump and leap distances are tripled.
