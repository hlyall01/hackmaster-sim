Bardic Knowledge (12 LP)
Bards tend to learn a little bit of everything. Because of this, bards sometimes know useless bits of trivia and other background history on just about any given topic (to wit, a 4% chance per level). The facts are only as correct or as complete as the GM’s secret percentile die rolls allow.

Bardic Music (10 BP)
The bard can use his music or poetics to inspire himself and his allies that can hear him perform. These talents can all be activated by reciting poetry, chanting, singing lyrical songs, singing melodies, humming, whistling or some other vocals, but the abilities are amplified if the bard plays an instrument along with his oral performance. Each individual use of bardic music may last up to five minutes. The bard cannot cast spells, speak, or use his voice for anything else without interrupting his bardic music. Playing an instrument along with his vocals requires both hands free, and the instrument may be stopped without ending the performance, as long as the bard continues vocalizing, but the instrumental bonus will be lost. It takes d4p+1 seconds to retrieve the instrument or stow it away. Each talent that requires Bardic Music grants the bard two additional uses of Bardic Music per day.

Dual Performance (30 BP)
requires level 18, Bardic Music, and any two other talents requiring Bardic Music
The bard can perform two different types of Bardic Music at once to provide the benefits of both at once. This counts as two uses of Bardic Music.

Inspire Courage (20 BP)
requires level 2 and Bardic Music
The bard can bolster against fear and improve combat abilities, granting himself and his allies +1 to their morale modifier, attack rolls and damage rolls. Using an instrument improves this by another +1. At 10th level, this improves to +2 (+3 with an instrument).

Inspire Competence (20 BP)
requires level 4 and Bardic Music
The bard can help an ally with a specific task. The bard grants one ally a +10% bonus on a specific skill for as long as they can hear the music. Certain skill uses cannot be bolstered with this ability. Using an instrument improves this by another +10%. At 12th level, this improves to +20% (+30% with an instrument).

Inspire Greatness (20 BP)
requires level 6 and Bardic Music
The bard can inspire greatness in himself and a number of allies equal to one third his level (rounded down), granting them +1 to their damage reduction. Using an instrument improves this by another +1. At 14th level, this improves to +2 (+3 with an instrument).

Inspire Heroics (20 BP)
requires level 8 and Bardic Music
The bard can inspire heroics in himself and his allies, granting a bonus of +1 to their physical, dodge and mental saving throw modifier, and defense bonus. Using an instrument improves this by another +1. At 16th level, this improves to +2 (+3 with instrument).
