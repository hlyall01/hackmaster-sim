**Natural Attunement (5 RP)**
You gain a -1 bonus to speed while in natural surroundings.

**Natural Protection (5 RP)**
You gain a +1 bonus to DR while in natural surroundings.

**Natural Awareness (5 RP)**
You gain a bonus of -2 to initiative while in natural surroundings.

**Warrior Woad (10 RP)**
_requires Average Artistry (painting)_
You may apply warpaint to yourself over the course of 10 minutes. Your blood produces a strange effect when coming into contact with such art (you must receive at least 1 point of damage from a piercing or hacking instrument). You add d6 points to your strength, and gain a bonus of +6 to defense, for 5d12p seconds. Upon expiration, however, both a physical and mental saving throw must be succeeded (against d20p+11), or the sudden release of power is too much to handle, causing you to fall unconscious for about an hour.
