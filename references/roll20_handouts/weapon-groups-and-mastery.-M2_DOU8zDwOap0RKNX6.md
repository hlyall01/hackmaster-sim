**Weapon Experience and Mastery:**
You do not need to be proficient with a weapon to gain weapon experience, but you cannot spend mastery points if you are unproficient. You gain a mastery point when reaching the weapon experience threshold for a weapon group as determined by your talents, intelligence and current weapon mastery tier with that group. Mastery points may be placed in Attack, Defense, Damage and Speed. One cannot acquire a +2 Damage bonus until first gaining the +1 bonus in all aspects. Additional weapon experience gained carries over toward earning the next mastery point.

Shields can now be mastered but only have two aspects (Defense and Speed). If using a shield, you use your Defense mastery from your shield instead of your weapon, even if it is worse, and you adjust your weapon speed by the lower value of your shield or weapon's Speed mastery.

The base weapon experience threshold is 100 for melee weapons, 150 for ranged weapons, and 200 for shields. Lower the threshold by 2% for each point of intelligence above 10, and increase the threshold by 2% for each point of intelligence below 10. Once an entire tier is mastered (reaching +1, +2, +3, etc in all aspects of the weapon group), the base weapon experience threshold for that group is increased by 20%. For example, the bow weapon groups would have a threshold of 180 once +1 in all aspects is reached, and 210 once +2 is reached. Certain talents may alter this further.

Gain 8d6p wexp per weapon type if you only used one weapon type in an encounter.
Gain 6d6p wexp per weapon type if you used two types of weapon in an encounter.
Gain 5d6p wexp per weapon type if you used three types of weapon in an encounter.
Gain 4d6p wexp per weapon type if you used four types of weapon in an encounter.
Gain 3d6p wexp per weapon type if you used five or more types of weapon in an encounter.

If an encounter was not against a competent opponent, receive no wexp.

Once a weapon group is at the maximum tier attainable, it no longer counts as being used in regards to gaining wexp.

Free Proficiency: For each entire tier mastered (+1, +2, +3, etc), gain a free proficiency of your choice within the same weapon group (except for shields). If you are already proficient with all weapons inside this weapon group, you may refund the BP of a previously purchased proficiency.

Weapon Types: Each weapon is now associated with a weapon group, and characters must master a group of weapons, instead of individual weapons. Becoming proficient and purchasing the martial talents which require a specified weapon are still done on an individual per-weapon basis.
