Requirements
>14+ Strength
>14+ Dexterity
>Must have at least +1 mastery with any weapon group.

Critical Mastery (specify weapon group) (30 BP)
requires level 15 and Improved Critical
The fighter is able to land critical strikes more often with his chosen weapon group, on a natural 18, 19 or 20.

Hardened (10 BP)
Fighters' Threshold of Pain is equal to 30% of their maximum hit points + another 2% per level (in place of the +1% per level less resilient characters receive). In addition, reduce your base Fatigue Factor by -2.

Unbreakable (30 BP)
requires 11th level and Tough Hide
The fighter improves the Damage Reduction bonus from Tough Hide by +1.

Weapon Specialization (specify weapon group) (20 BP)
requires Weapon Focus with the specified weapon group
The fighter gains +2d6p on weapon experience dice rolls with the chosen group of weapons.

Weapon Supremacy (specify weapon group) (50 BP)
requires Weapon Specialization with the specified weapon group
Increase the maximum tier of mastery from +5 to +6 with the chosen group of weapons.

Whirlwind Mastery (25 BP)
requires level 9 and Whirlwind Attack
The fighter is able to use the Whirlwind Attack maneuver with no speed penalty. Additionally, at level 15, defenders of the Whirlwind Attack always receive full damage, even if they Give Ground or Scamper Back.

Wounding Criticals (specify weapon group) (20 BP)
requires level 11 and Improved Critical
The fighter is able to deal deadlier critical strikes with his chosen weapon group, increasing Wound Severity by 3 on his critical hits. This talent may be purchased twice, increasing Wound Severity with his chosen weapon group by another 3 to a total of 6.
