_description:_ typically medium in height with slender to wiry builds. skin tones range from pale gray to deep umber, although sometimes reds and deep browns occur, often with a fine, velvety texture. hair is usually dark and sparse, though some individuals are completely hairless. eyes are large and expressive, commonly black, red-brown, or amber. facial features are sharp and narrow, with prominent ears and short muzzles.
the flightless strain lack wings entirely, with heavier, more grounded builds and broader chests. arms are proportioned like a typical humanoid’s, and features tend to be less elongated, giving them a more solid, terrestrial appearance.
the feral strain possess broad, membranous wings extending from elongated arms to the flanks, with reinforced shoulders and lighter frames. posture tends to be slightly hunched, and hands often retain long, hooked fingers.
the flighted strain possess fully separated wings and arms, with wings emerging from the shoulder blades rather than the arms themselves. their wings are large and powerful, supported by elongated, jointed bones and layered membranes. frames are lighter and more elongated than the flightless strain, but sturdier and more upright than the feral strain, balancing aerial capability with dexterity. the chest and back are strongly muscled, especially around the shoulders and spine, giving them a broad, angular silhouette. arms remain mostly humanoid in proportion and function, ending in clawed hands.



_plural:_ cirodes
_other common names:_ bats, bone collectors, eutherians, night terrors
