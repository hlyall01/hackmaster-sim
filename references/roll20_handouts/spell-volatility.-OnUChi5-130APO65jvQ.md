**Volatility Save:** When a magic user casts a spell that has been amplified with essence, they may have to make a saving throw, a d20p and plus the associated ability score against the appropriate threshold below. No roll is necessary if the magic user's associated ability score is high enough to automatically succeed, as a die result of 1 is not an automatic failure.

**Injuries while Casting:** If a magic user is struck while casting a spell that requires a volatility save, apply the worst of the following penalties to their saving throw roll. If any injuries suffered prevent a magic user from continuing to cast, the volatility save is rolled immediately to determine mishaps (even if the spell does not go off). Magic users with the Combat Casting talent suffer a penalty as if their injury were one category weaker.

_-4_ Struck by blow absorbed by DR or deflected by shield
_-7_ Suffers a wound
_-9_ Suffers a knock-back
_-11_ Successful Trauma save
_-15_ Failed Trauma save
_Automatic Tier 4 Spell Mishap_ Knocked unconscious or dropped to 0 hit points
_Automatic Tier 6 Spell Mishap_ Reduced to negative hp
_Automatic Tier 8 Spell Mishap_ Caster killed outright

**_Failure by 1_** Executes the spell at baseline capability.
**Failure by 2** The spell fails and all essence points expended are lost.
**Failure by 3** A tier 1 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 4** A tier 2 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 5** A tier 3 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 6** A tier 4 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 7** A tier 5 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 8** A tier 6 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 9** A tier 7 spell mishap occurs.
**Failure by 10+** A tier 8 spell mishap occurs.
