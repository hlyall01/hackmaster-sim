**Huntsman (5 RP)**
_requires Average Hunting_
If you are not engaged in melee, you may take 5 seconds to designate a single creature you can see as your quarry. You gain a +1 to hit your quarry, and tracking them is 15% easier until you next rest or designate another target.

**Huntsmaster (5 RP)**
_requires Advanced Hunting_
The benefits you receive when targeting your quarry are doubled, and you may have two quarries simultaneously. You may choose which quarry to drop attention from, if you already have two quarries, when attempting to designate a new creature.

**Ysgrewng (5 RP)**
_requires Expert Fymblwngish_
The Ysgrewng is the signature battle roar of the Fymblwngen people. Letting loose this cry at the onset of a battle intimidates all intelligent, humanoid opponents within a 25 foot radius. Those who hear it must make an immediate morale check (excepting other Fymblwngen). Those that fail have their morale level reduced by one increment, and cowardly opponents flee on failure.

**Defiant (10 RP)**
When an enemy scores a critical hit against you, they must roll their damage twice and take the weaker result. This occurs before determining critical hit severity and additional effects.
