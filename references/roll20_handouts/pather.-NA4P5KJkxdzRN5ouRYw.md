**Educated (5 RP)**
You receive a free purchase of any two different skills where intelligence is the sole relevant attribute at each level. This is retroactive.

**Pedestrian (5 RP)**
Walking long distances on foot has strengthened your back, increasing the amount of weight you can carry before being considered lightly or moderately encumbered by 50% (rounded down).

**Pilgrim (5 RP)**
You have walked the Paths, and as such, your resilience to hardship has increased. Your recovery time upon failing a trauma check is only 2 seconds times the difference between the ½ Con check and the ½ Con score. Additionally, you only fall unconscious for d6p minutes after rolling a 20 on trauma checks.

**Armored to the Teeth (10 RP)**
_requires heavy armor proficiency_
You are able to convert the weight of your armor into momentum, and as such you gain +1 to your damage modifier while wearing heavy armor.
