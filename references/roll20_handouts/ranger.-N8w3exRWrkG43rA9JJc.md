Requirements
>13+ Wisdom
>13+ Dexterity
>13+ Constitution
>At least one ranged weapon proficiency
>Skinning/Tanning Proficiency
>Average mastery in Hunting
>Average mastery in Tracking
>Average mastery in Survival

Ambush (15 BP)
The ranger may launch a special attack with a ranged or throwing weapon at an opponent unaware of his presence. This attack inflicts penetration damage on both the maximum die roll and said roll -1. Upon reaching 17th level, this attack inflicts penetration damage on both the maximum die roll, said roll -1, and said roll -2.

Beastmaster (20 LP)
Rangers with this talent gain one free roll of both Animal Empathy and Animal Training with the appropriate mastery die at each level. This talent is retroactive and will grant an amount of rolls for both Animal Empathy and Animal Training equal to your current level upon purchase.

Camouflage (25 BP)
The ranger receives a +50 bonus to hiding and sneaking skill checks in natural surroundings.

Hardened (10 BP)
Rangers' Threshold of Pain is equal to 30% of their maximum hit points + another 2% per level (in place of the +1% per level less resilient characters receive). In addition, reduce your base Fatigue Factor by -2.

Pathfinder (8 BP)
The ranger receives a third attempt to find a trail, instead of only having two. At levels 12 and 18 he receives a fourth and fifth attempt respectively.

Ranged Critical Mastery (specify weapon group) (30 BP)
requires level 15 and Improved Critical with a ranged weapon
The ranger is able to land critical strikes more often with ranged weapons within his chosen weapon group, on a natural 18, 19 or 20.

Ranged Weapon Specialization (specify weapon group) (20 BP)
requires Weapon Focus with a weapon group that contains a ranged weapon
The ranger gains +2d6p on weapon experience dice rolls with ranged weapons within the chosen group.

Ranged Weapon Supremacy (specify weapon group) (50 BP)
requires Ranged Weapon Specialization with the specified weapon group
Increase the maximum tier of mastery from +5 to +6 with ranged weapons within the chosen group.

Slayer of Beasts (15 BP)
On a successful hit, rangers gain a bonus of +1 to damage for every three full experience levels attained against non-fantastical animals.

Slayer of Foes (20 BP)
On a successful hit, rangers gain a bonus of +1 to damage for every three full experience levels attained against monstrous humanoids.

Slayer of Monsters (25 BP)
requires Slayer of Beasts and Slayer of Foes
On a successful hit, rangers gain a bonus of +1 to damage for every three full experience levels attained against fantastical animals and monsters.

Swift Tracker (8 BP)
The ranger only needs 20 minutes to pick up a trail. At 15th level, only 10 minutes are required to find a trail.
