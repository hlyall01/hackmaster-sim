Weapon styles cannot be purchased, instead they must be taught to your character (usually via a few weeks of sparring with the same tutor or studying and practicing a treatise extensively).

You can only use one weapon style at a time, and you may only use a weapon style if wielding one of the required weapons (or both, if the talent requires multiple proficiencies). You may change between weapon styles or cease using a weapon style upon either readying a weapon, engaging in melee, or upon your next attack opportunity.

**Armeroci Pole**
_requires proficiency with any large sword or polearm with at least 5 feet of reach_
The Armeroci do not simply wield weapons, they draw circles of death around themselves, and dare others to cross the line.
Your reach is increased by 1 foot and you deal an extra die of damage on your initial engagement strikes at the cost of increasing your weapon speed by 2. In addition, you always receive an initial engagement strike against foes engaging you, as long as your reach is equivalent or greater than theirs (even if you are already engaged).

**Crescent Moon**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of small sword and size M large sword_
Strike to unbalance and disarm, turn every attack into an opening for a counter.
When two-weapon fighting defensively with a primary large sword and secondary small sword, your perfect and near-perfect defenses allow you to immediately called shot your opponent's weapon (granting them a +6 to their defense) instead of dealing damage. If you hit, your opponent's weapon is flung 2d6p feet in a random direction (results of 4 feet or less mean the weapon falls to the ground beneath them). You also treat natural 18s as near-perfect defenses, or 17s with the Superior Defense talent.

**Doomrazor**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of piercing melee weapon_
It is an art, leaving wounds that seem shallow at first, until the victim realizes too late that the bleeding is happening where no bandage can reach.
Do not add your strength or mastery bonus to damage, and no longer penetrate. In exchange, each wound you inflict is accompanied by internal hemorrhaging.

**Falling Sun**
_requires flamberge or two-handed sword proficiency_
It is said that this technique was taught to man by a being forged of light who, shortly after, ascended into the night sky.
At the cost of increasing your weapon speed by 2, your attack, defense and damage rolls now penetrate on both the maximum die roll and said roll -1.

**Fymblwnger**
_requires battle axe, executioner's axe or greataxe proficiency
_Said to originate from giants, warriors using this style are taught to swing as though hurling mountains.
Your opponents are no longer afforded a bonus to defense when they Give Ground or Scamper Back.

**Hammerer**
_requires greathammer, hammer, maul or warhammer proficiency_
Bodies crumpling with each blow, reminding them that resistance has a cost.
Your knock-backs always reset your opponent's weapon count.

**Hobbler**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of polearm or spear_
If you can't move, you cannot fight.
You suffer a -4 penalty to attack, but roll a d2000 for each strike you land. You deal the effects of a critical strike in that location according to the severity of the blow, although without the extra damage dice.

**Ithican Prince**
_requires shield proficiency and proficiency in at least one type of small sword_
The merchant princes of Ithica prize cleverness above strength. Every step a feint, every strike a lesson.
Your intelligence grants half its attack bonus to both damage and defense while using a buckler and small sword.

**Quiet River**
_requires fist proficiency_
A quiet river carves mountains.
You deal half damage, but ignore all DR (natural, armor, shield, or otherwise). While not wearing armor or using a shield, your base defense with your bare hands is d20p and you receive double the defense bonus from unarmed weapons mastery. You are not considered armed, however armed opponents do not receive free counterattacks against you.

**Regenstat**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size M small or large sword_
The Vetlander sword-duelists train to read the slightest hint of intent.
When engaged with a single opponent while using your sword two-handed or one-handed without an offhand, gain +1 to attack and defense each time you successfully attack or defend (max +8), resetting upon missing or being hit.

**Returner**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size L large sword_
Conceived by a warrior-king tired of bloodshed, many wars were settled in single combat with this gambit.
Subject yourself to a -4 penalty to your defense and in exchange you may take advantage of your opponent's exposed defenses to attack them as they attack you. You may only do this return attack against one enemy attack between your own attack rolls, but you still suffer the defense penalty against subsequent attacks. In addition, you may forgo your initial attack when engaging and instead deal double damage on your retaliatory attack.

Rhdwng Flow
requires proficiency in at least one type of throwing weapon
Rhdwngers say that force does not begin in the body, but in the earth itself.
You may add your full strength bonus to thrown weapons (if applicable), no matter the speed at which you move.

**Rohavalan Bridge**
_requires proficiency in staves or at least one type of polearm that grants a full d20p for defense_
A ceaseless cascade, one blow flowing into another.
Halve your modified weapon speed, minimum weapon speed and reach. However, you must use a 2d4p in place of your weapon's normal damage die if your attack exceeds your opponent's defense by less than 10.

**Scorn of the Dissendri**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size S melee weapon_
Two hands, one will.
When two-weapon fighting (offensively or defensively), strike with both weapons on the initial engagement hits, perfect defenses and near-perfect defenses.

**Shield of Blades**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size S or M sword with at least 2 feet of reach_
Be not a man, but a wall wrought of whirling iron.
When wielding two one-handed swords defensively, you may use a full d20p plus any defense bonus from both the primary and secondary weapons for all defense rolls against frontal and flank attackers. This bonus may additionally be used against ranged attackers, provided you can see the approaching missile. If you possess the Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting talent, you may use this style and Storm of Blades simultaneously.

Six Paths
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size M large sword and shields_
There's an odd calmness to this style. Parry, cut, bash, over and over.
When wielding a large sword and either a medium or small shield, your shield is only hit within 5 of the defense roll (instead of 10). Furthermore, each successful strike you land permits you a free shield bash attack on the following second.

Storm of Blades
requires proficiency in at least one type of size S or M sword with at least 2 feet of reach
A relentless cascade of steel, sure to turn any foe into mist.
When wielding two one-handed swords offensively, the initial attack of your secondary weapon is always made exactly two seconds after your initial primary attack. Additionally, your subsequent attacks (both primary and secondary) are delayed one second less. If you possess the Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting talent, you may use this style and Shield of Blades simultaneously.
**
Three Mountains**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of crushing melee weapon_
West of Caernoth lies a monastery hidden within this technique's namesake, what it has to do with it, I do not know.
If you strike the same creature three times in a row without missing, it must make a Physical Saving Throw against your most recent adjusted attack roll or automatically take 20 on its next Trauma save.

**Twelve Paths**
_requires proficiency in at least one type of size L large sword and shields_
Ask a dozen Twelve Paths students how to defend yourself, and you'll get a dozen different answers. It’s all very philosophical until you have to fight one.
At the cost of a -3 penalty to damage, you are able to wield both a size L large sword and either a small shield or buckler in conjunction, combining your defense modifiers from both your weapon and shield against all attackers in your arc of defense. In addition, your next attack comes two seconds sooner each time an opponent strikes your shield (immediately attacking if this reduces your weapon count to the current second).

**Unbreakable Wall**
_requires shield proficiency_
The blow meets the shield, and dies there.
Large or tower shields you wield gain +2 DR. You know how to lean your body into your shield, reducing the damage dealt to your shield by its DR (for the purposes of breakage).
