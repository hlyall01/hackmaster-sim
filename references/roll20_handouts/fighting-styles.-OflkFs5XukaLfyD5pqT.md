**Weapon and shield:**
_Attack:_ Attack normally.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d20p plus shield bonus and other modifiers. An attack roll below (but within 10 of) your defense roll hits your shield for shield damage (generally halved damage dice). This may trigger breakage. This damage is then reduced by your shield's DR and then received as normal.

**Two-handed weapon:**
_Attack:_ Attack normally.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d20p plus modifiers against one enemy attack between your own attack rolls. Second and succeeding defense rolls during this period are at d20p-4 plus modifiers.

**One-handed weapon (one-handed):**
_Attack:_ Focusing on only one weapon provides a -1 speed bonus.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d20p-4 plus modifiers.

**One-handed weapon (two-handed):**
_Attack:_ Gain +3 damage but receive a penalty of +2 to speed.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d20p plus modifiers against one enemy attack between your own attack rolls. Second and succeeding defense rolls during this period are at d20p-4 plus modifiers.

**Two one-handed weapons (offensive):**
_Attack:_ Initial attack happens with the primary weapon as normal. Subsequent attacks with the primary weapon are made on its weapon speed plus 2. The initial attack with the secondary weapon occurs two seconds after the primary weapon, plus half the primary weapon's speed. Subsequent attacks with the secondary weapon occur at the secondary weapon's speed plus two. The secondary weapon receives a -2 damage penalty.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d10p plus primary weapon's defense bonus plus any other normal modifiers. Near-perfect defense damage is full if the secondary weapon is smaller than the wielder.

**Two one-handed weapons (defensive):**
_Attack:_ Attack normally with the primary weapon.
_Defense:_ Base defense roll of d20p plus both the primary and secondary weapon's defense bonuses plus any other normal modifiers against one enemy attack between your primary weapon's attack rolls. Second and succeeding defense rolls during this period use a d20p-4 with the same modifiers. Near-perfect defense damage is full if the secondary weapon is smaller than the wielder.

**Shield only:**
_Attack:_ May only attack unarmed or via shield bash.
_Defense:_ +1 bonus to defense and opponents can attack at half their weapon speed, otherwise as weapon and shield.

**Two shields:**
_Attack:_ May only shield bash.
_Defense:_ As weapon and shield, but both shield bonuses may be used against frontal attackers, a single shield's bonus may be used against flanking attackers, and opponents can attack at half their weapon speed.
