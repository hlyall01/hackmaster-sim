**Groin Stomp (5 RP)**
You can perform this attack only on opponents who are already prone, who have been knocked to the ground, or who are sleeping. If this precondition is met, you can jump onto or kick your opponent (the stomp need not actually be in the groin). A successful attack hits with sufficient force to stun your opponent for 2d8p seconds and cause d4 points of damage per stomp. While the opponent is on the ground, you can continue to perform this maneuver, causing additional damage every 10 seconds, until your opponent is unconscious. This particularly vicious form of attack also causes the victim to lose a point of Honor.

**Project Confidence (5 RP)**
You can put up a front of overwhelming confidence when you Aggressive Attack. Giving Ground no longer results in half damage for your Aggressive Attacks, only Scampering Back. In addition, you gain another +1 to hit while Aggressive Attacking (for a total of +6).

**Raconteur (5 RP)**
_requires 13+ Charisma_
You gain a free purchase of both Distraction and Fast Talking at each level. This is retroactive.

**Edge Counter (10 RP)**
When you land a riposte via Perfect Defense you automatically critically hit.
