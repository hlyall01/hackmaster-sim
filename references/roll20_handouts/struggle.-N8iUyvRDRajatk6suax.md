**Essence:** Exertion

Feat of Strength
**Spell Level:** 1
**Base EP Cost:** 60
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 1 minute
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Duration: 5 EP/5 seconds
Increase Strength: 10 EP/50 fractional points

The target can attempt one action requiring great physical strength. They perform this action as if they possess 18/00 Strength. The spell expires after one action. This spell is typically used to perform Feats of Strength, but can be used in combat (for a single attack), or to drag something for a moment.

Springing
**Spell Level:** 2
**Base EP Cost:** 70
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 2 minutes
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Distance: 5 EP/10% distance
Increase Duration: 10 EP/minute

This spell endows an individual with gravity-defying jumping abilities. For vertical jumps, they can ascend up to 10 feet off the ground. They can also spring up to 10 feet backward or bound up to 20 feet forward from a standstill. If they get a jogging or running start of at least 10 feet, they can leap up to 30 feet.

Tireless Run
**Spell Level:** 3
**Base EP Cost:** 80
**Components:** S
**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 4 hours
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Duration: 10 EP/hour

The target creature can move at its run speed for 4 hours without becoming fatigued. Once the duration ends, the creature is physically exhausted and must rest, eat, and hydrate for the same amount of time that it ran before it can perform even mildly strenuous activity.

Wall Walk
**Spell Level:** 4
**Base EP Cost:** 90
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 5 minutes
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Duration: 15 EP/minute

The target gains the ability to ascend or descend sheer surfaces at a rate of 2½ feet per second. They do this with their feet flat on the wall, body perpendicular to the surface. The wall cannot be sharper than a 90° angle or the recipient falls to the ground.

Endure Elements
**Spell Level:** 5
**Base EP Cost:** 100
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 4 hours
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Duration: 10 EP/30 minutes
Increase Targets: 25 EP/target (must be within 5 feet of the original target)
Increase Heat Protection: 25 EP/+10° and +1 DR against heat
Increase Cold Protection: 25 EP/-10° and +1 DR against cold

The recipient can easily endure unusually high temperatures (up to 60° Celsius) without breaking a sweat, and comfortably survive in cold environments (down to -30° Celsius), feeling nothing more than a mild chill. If the temperature goes beyond the spell's efficacy in one direction, the protection ceases for that side, but the protection remains for the other end.

If the target is attacked by magical heat or cold (e.g. spells or breath weapons), that side of the spell's protection ends, but provides 3 DR against the effect.

Skin Breathe
**Spell Level:** 7
**Base EP Cost:** 120
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 10 seconds
**Range:** Touch
**Area of Effect:** One creature
**Duration**: 1 hour
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Duration: 20 EP/hour
Increase Targets: 60 EP/target (must be within 5 feet of the original target)

The recipient's skin becomes permeable to gasses. If submerged in water, the individual will not asphyxiate as they have the ability to transdermally absorb dissolved oxygen in a manner similar to amphibians.

This power carries a liability in the form of increased susceptibility to noxious or poisonous gasses. If exposed to harmful vapors while on dry land, any saving throws are made with a -6 penalty.
