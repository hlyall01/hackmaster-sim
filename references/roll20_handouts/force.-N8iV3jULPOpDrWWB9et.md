_Repel_
**Spell Level:** 1
**Base EP Cost:** 60
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** 30 feet
**Area of Effect:** One target
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** Physical negates
**Amplification:**
Increase Force: 15 EP/100 pounds
Increase Range: 5 SP/5 feet

The caster creates a force that pushes against a desired creature or object. The force is sufficient to move a 200-pound object up to a distance of 1 foot. Lighter objects can be pushed further, heavier ones less so. For example, a 100 pound object may be moved two feet while a 400 pound object may only be moved half a foot.

Creatures may make a saving throw to resist and negate the effect of the spell. If the spell is cast against a creature's weapon, they suffer a -1 penalty on their next defense or attack roll (whichever occurs first and within 3 seconds). Objects secured in place (such as a locked door) are unaffected by this spell.

Launching an object at a target requires an attack roll, and deals a number of d3p (rounded up) in damage equal to the weight of the object divided by 200, multiplied by the remaining distance in feet. The resulting damage is reduced by DR. Particularly hard or sharp objects will deal a number of d4ps instead. Particularly soft objects will only do this damage for the purposes of knock-backs.

Break Portal
**Spell Level:** 2
**Base EP Cost:** 70
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** 30 feet
**Area of Effect:** One portal
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** None
**Amplification:**
Increase Range: 5 EP/10 feet

The caster hurls a tremendous force against a door, hatch, window or similar portal. A closed (and even locked and barred) portal is automatically forced open, accompanied by the noise one would expect from a battering ram. The force of this spell is only effective against portals, and has no effect on walls or creatures.

This spell counters Bar Portal, but the caster must succeed a contested check against a portal secured with Lock.

Fracture
**Spell Level:** 3
**Base EP Cost:** 80
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 2 seconds
**Range:** 180 feet
**Area of Effect:** One object weighing up to 10 lbs
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** None
Amplification:
Increase Weight: 10 EP/1 lb

The caster causes one object (weighing no more than 10 lbs) to develop stress fractures that compromise its structural integrity. Weapons crack on the next blow, bowstrings snap, armor tears apart when struck, shields are cleaved, locks give way, chains are broken, ladders collapse, glass shatters. Magic items are unaffected by this spell.

Missile
**Spell Level:** 4
**Base EP Cost:** 90
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 second
**Range:** 180 feet
**Area of Effect:** One to seven targets in a 20 foot square
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** None
Amplification:
Increase Range: 5 EP/5 feet
Additional Projectiles: 40 EP/missile

The caster shoots an unerringly accurate glowing projectile of magical energy at an opponent. Provided any portion of the target can be seen, the missile will home around obstacles to strike its target. The missile inflicts d4p+1 damage. With amplification, multiple projectiles may be fired, with the caster able to direct them at several targets or volley fire them onto a single one. Multiple missiles impacting a creature strike simultaneously but count as individual wounds.

Bash Face
**Spell Level:** 5
**Base EP Cost:** 100
**Components:** S
**Casting Time:** 3 seconds
**Range:** 15 feet
**Area of Effect:** One target
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** None
Amplification:
Increase Range: 20 EP/5 feet
Increase Damage: 50 EP/d6p damage
Attack Bonus: 20 EP/+1 attack

The caster winds up a haymaker, bashing the target (usually in the face) with a strong force. The caster must succeed at an attack roll to land the punch, but the target must use a d20p-4 defense value as the blow originates only a few inches from their body. If successful, the haymaker deals 4d6p damage and can cause knock-backs.

Stay Down
**Spell Level:** 6
**Base EP Cost:** 110
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 1 seconds
**Range:** 60 feet
**Area of Effect:** 10 foot radius
**Duration:** 10 seconds
**Saving Throw:** Physical negates (see below)
Amplification:
Increase Duration: 10 EP/second
Increase Radius: 25 EP/5 feet

The caster creates an oppressive force in the area. Standing up from the ground takes an extra second, and both crawling and standing require a successful Physical save (for the latter, the save is made on the final second of standing).

Impaling Missile
**Spell Level:** 7
**Base EP Cost:** 120
**Components:** V, S
**Casting Time:** 2 seconds
**Range:** 90 feet
**Area of Effect:** Two or more targets
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Saving Throw:** None
Amplification:
Increase Damage: 40 EP/d4p+1 damage
Increase Range: 5 EP/10 feet

The caster creates a glowing projectile of magical energy that is directed toward a target within range. The missile always hits and causes 2d4p+2 damage. When the missile strikes, it skewers its target, inflicting its damage before it continues along its path, until it strikes an obstacle, runs out of range, or runs out of targets.

When the missile strikes its first target, it emerges out of the opposite side and is directed toward an additional target of the caster's choice within a 90° arc (i.e. it may veer 45° left or right). The first creature struck suffers full damage, but each additional creature struck suffers d4p+1 less damage than its predecessor. The projectile continues until it runs out of energy, range, or cannot continue due to barriers in its path.
