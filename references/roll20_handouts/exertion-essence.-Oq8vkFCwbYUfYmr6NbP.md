**Casting Abilities:** Strength, Constitution
**
Generation:** Whenever you are knocked back or knock another creature back, gain 5 exertion essence per level you possess for every 5 feet launched. Additionally, after failing a save (physical, dodge, mental, trauma, volatility, or death) gain essence based on how close you were to success according to the following table:

**Failed By** | 1-5 | 6-10 | 11+ | 16+ |
**Essence** | 20 per level | 10 per level | 5 per level | 0 |
**Unbound Impetus:** Each Force spell cast must be maximally amplified to 300%, or as close as possible if lacking essence or proficiency. If your essence ever goes beyond its maximum capacity, immediately knock-back everything surrounding you. The force of the knock-back is d6p per 100 essence you had (including over maximum) to everything within 60 feet, and half the force up to 60 feet beyond that. This does not grant you essence.
