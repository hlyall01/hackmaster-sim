Higher tier materials grant the following bonuses.
Melee and Thrown weapons have increased Attack and Damage.
Bows and Slings have increased Damage and require 1 additional Strength per tier.
Crossbows have increased Damage per tier.
Projectiles have increased Attack and Damage (this stacks with the ranged weapon used to fire them).
Armors have increased Damage Reduction and reduced Defense penalty (+0 max).
Shields have increased Damage Reduction and Defense.
