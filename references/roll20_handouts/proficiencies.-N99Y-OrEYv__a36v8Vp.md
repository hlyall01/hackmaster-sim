**Minimal Skill Weapon (1 BP)**
These weapons can be used by anyone without any formal training and very little practice. These weapons are so easy to use that characters receive a mere penalty of 1 to attack, defense, speed and damage when using a weapon untrained.
_Minimal Skill Weapons: antler, cestus, claw, club, fang, fist, gauntlet, spiked gauntlet_

**Low Skill Weapon (2 BP)**
Proper use of these weapons is not intuitive but the proper techniques are relatively easy to learn if instructed by a knowledgeable trainer. These weapons are relatively easy to use; characters receive a penalty of 2 to attack, defense, speed and damage when using these weapons untrained (attack and speed only for ranged weapons).
_Low Skill Weapons: antler, arbalest, bardiche, battle axe, dagger, fauchard, flail, gladius, glaive, greataxe, greatclub, greathammer, guisarme, hammer, handaxe, hand crossbow, hasta, heavy crossbow, khopesh, knife, javelin, light crossbow, mace, maul, military pick, morningstar, partisan, pike, pilum, scourge, short spear, short sword, sickle, spear, spetum, staff, voulge, warhammer, whip_

**Medium Skill Weapon (4 BP)**
These weapons require a good deal more training to master. Proper use requires length practice and familiarization with the weapon in actual sparring or extended practice to master the technique (in the case of thrown weapons). Using these weapons untrained imposes a penalty of 4 to attack, defense, speed and damage because they are more difficult to master (attack and speed only for ranged weapons).
_Medium Skill Weapons: bastard sword, bola, broadsword, claymore, dart, executioner's axe, flamberge, halberd, horseman's flail, horseman's mace, horseman's pick, greatknife, greatsword, lance, longsword, net, poleaxe, polehammer, ranseur, raven's beak, sabre, scimitar, scythe, shortbow, sling, spatha, spiked chain, swordstaff, throwing axe, thrusting sword, trident, two-handed sword_

**High Skill Weapon (6 BP)**
These finesse weapons require years of practice to master. Characters receive a penalty of 6 to attack, defense, speed and damage when using these untrained (attack and speed only for ranged weapons).
_High Skill Weapons: double axe, double scimitar, dual scythe, hooked hammer, lajatang, lasso, longbow, mancatcher, recurve bow, spear-axe, two-bladed sword, warbow_

**Light Armor (3 BP)**
A character with this proficiency knows how to don and remove light armor, maintain it, and fight while restricted by it.

**Medium Armor (3 BP)**
_requires Light Armor proficiency_
A character with this proficiency knows how to don and remove medium armor, maintain it, and fight while restricted by it.

**Heavy Armor (4 BP)**
_requires Medium Armor proficiency_
A character with this proficiency knows how to don and remove heavy armor, maintain it, and fight while restricted by it.

**Shields (6 BP)**
Effectively employing a shield is a proficiency that must be learned through rigorous practice. Through frequent sparring, the character learns the best methods of deflecting an opponent's blows and strengthens his wrist, forearm and shoulder.

**Bilingual (5 LP)**
This proficiency, which may only be purchased during character creation, provides the character with a second native language (i.e. mastery of 37+2x INT+d20p). In general, this language should be that of a neighbouring region, or one with which the juvenile character may have had extensive exposure to. This proficiency may be purchased repeatedly, effectively becoming Trilingual and so forth.

**Hiking/Roadmarching (4 LP)**
_requires 11+ Constitution_
Those proficient in road marching may cover 25 miles per day in open terrain, 15 miles per day in impeded terrain, and 8 miles in restricted terrain. Such characters may attempt to push their physical endurance by exceeding these limits. In so doing, they may march up to 30 miles (or 18 in impeded and 9 in restricted terrain). However, they will exhaust themselves and suffer a -1 penalty to attack, defense, and damage that remains in effect until they gain the benefit of a full day's rest. A second consecutive day's forced march adds a -2 penalty and a third -3 (i.e. 3 continuous days of forced marching accumulates a -6 penalty). However, if forced marches occur only on alternating days, each such march affords only a -1 penalty (i.e. after six days of on-off forced marching, each person has accumulated a -3 penalty). Only one full day of rest is required regardless of the penalties accrued.

**Laborer (1 LP)**
Persons with this proficiency are able to meaningfully contribute to some labor-intensive group task such as constructing a castle wall, harvesting a crop, or mining ore. They need not be familiar with the task as long as they are supervised by someone who is. Persons without this skill cannot add their labor to group tasks (they simply get in the way, ask too many questions, and distract those actually doing something useful).

**Maintenance/Upkeep (5 LP)**
A person with this proficiency is generally handy with tools. He is a general all-around fix-it man. He can keep dwellings in good repair, as well as most equipment needed for adventuring. He can also making minor repairs to wagons and carts. This is all dependent, of course, on whether he has the proper tools or not. So long as the character has the equipment, time, and money, he is able to successfully complete these tasks. Characters with this proficiency receive a +2 bonus to all competing rolls when determining breakage.

**Peg Leg (10 BP)**
Becoming proficient in the use of a prosthetic limb is not an easy task. However, for those suffering the catastrophic loss of a leg, the arduous rehabilitation process is well worth the effort to restore mobility. While using a pegleg is infinitely better than the alternative of being carted around by assistants (or crawling), it is no substitute for a functional leg. Running and Sprinting are no longer possible, and one invariably loses the ability to engage in deft footwork. For combatants, this translates to a permanent -2 penalty to Attack and Defense.

**Formation Fighting (5 BP)**
Fighting in massed ranks is a very effective means for infantry to concentrate force against an opponent. It allows others besides the foremost rank in contact with the enemy to bring their weapons to bear. There are, of course, limitations. The second rank may only employ weapons with over 5 feet of reach and can only attack via the Jab special combat move. The third rank may also only attack via the Jab special move and must have over 10 feet of reach. The fourth rank is subject to the same limitations except the pike is the only permissible weapon. Without training, all frontline combatants incur a -2 attack penalty and +1 speed penalty, as they must be every wary of the unseen sharp implements sporadically entering their fighting zone. Those in the rear ranks are more severly hampered, as they must seek to avoid impaling their ally while he bobs and weaves in an unpredictable manner and invariably inserts himself self directly between the enemy and those behind him. These individuals suffer a -6 attack penalty and a +4 speed penalty. In addition, on any attack roll of ≤6 on the d20p that fails to hit the target, or on any fumble, they impale their frontline ally for full damage. This fumble result either occurs in tandem with ancillary fumble effects, or supercedes the effect as appropriate. Those with appropriate training may operate with far greater precision. However, this proficiency is synergistic and requires all ranks to possess the proficiency. The benefit accrued to the frontline combatant is absolutely dependant on the ally to his rear possessing the ability to operate in synch. Thus unless all ranks possess the proficiency, no benefit applies. When operating as part of a trained formation, frontline warriors incur no penalties, and those to their rear suffer a mere -2 Attack and +2 speed penalty. There remains a chance that they may accidentally impale those to their front, but only on a fumble.

**Rigger (5 LP)**
_requires Average Climbing and Advanced Rope Use_
This proficiency is essential for handling a rigged ship as without it the sails cannot be effectively employed to utilize the wind for motive power. Riggers know how to rig a ship while underway (rigging the stun'sls; rigging the braces, lifts, tacks, sheets, clews, and bunts; how to raise and lower heavy sails; how to bend sails to the spars and reeve the running rigging; how to box haul or club haul the ship).

**Seaman's Cant (1 LP)**
This proficiency provides the user with expanded maritime vocabulary and jargon. This includes visual familiary with all naval technology and the ability to effectively communicate with sailors in regards to the myriad of tasks that must be performed to sail and maintain a blue water vessel.

**Skinning/Tanning (2 LP)**
This proficiency involves removing the skin from a creature with a minimum amount of damage. The character must have a sharp knife and the dead creature in order to perform this act. A character can also tan hides with this proficiency, provided that he has appropriate tools (e.g., knives, mallets, vats, etc.), the various noxious tanning materials and solutions required (e.g., animal brains, lime, cedar, alum, dung, urine, and water), and enough time. It often takes hours or days just for the cleaning and soaking alone.

**Taxidermy (4 LP)**
_requires Skinning/Tanning, Advanced Leatherworking and Novice Carpentry_
A character with this proficiency knows how to stuff and mount dead creatures in a realistic display. The taxidermist can perform this work on all types of vertebrate amphibians, birds, mammals, reptiles, as well monsters that generally fit into one or more of these categories. Average completion time depends on the size. As a rough guide, small creatures take about 3 weeks, while something as large as a grizzly bear may take a month and a half.
