Fatigue is not checked for the first combat of a given day, as the adrenaline eliminates any potential tiredness at that point. Further, you need not check fatigue during the first 10 seconds of any engagement. Surprised characters need not check for fatigue, either.

When you make a defense roll in melee, if the raw result of the dice is equal to or under your current Fatigue Factor, you become winded and suffer a penalty of 1 to your Attack, Defense, Damage and Speed. Each subsequent failure doubles this penalty to 2, then 4, then 8 and so on. Defense rolls against ranged attacks do not trigger fatigue. Characters with zero or lower current Fatigue Factor only suffer fatigue penalties on natural 1s. Taking a knee and resting for a full 10 seconds reduces your fatigue penalty by 1. Five minutes of complete rest eliminates all fatigue penalties. Determine your Fatigue Factor via the following tables.
