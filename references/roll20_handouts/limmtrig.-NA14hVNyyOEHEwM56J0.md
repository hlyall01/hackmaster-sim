**Gentle Tumble (5 RP)**
You relax your muscles instinctually when you fall, allowing you to fall twice as far. You also no longer suffer damage from falls of up to 10 feet.

**Jack (5 RP)**
You gain an additional 3 LP per level. This is retroactive.

**Sapdrinker (5 RP)**
Tree sap vitalizes your body like no other form of nourishment. Each day you consume a pint of sap, you will heal an additional 1 point to each of your wounds.

**Sapblood (10 RP)**
_requires Sapdrinker_
Tree sap improves your body's processes to such an extent that your broken bones will heal, and you are even able to regrow limbs (over the course of about a week), as long as you are able to maintain the diet. In addition, you will heal an additional 1 point to each of your wounds each day you consume a pint of sap.

**Stumbling Gait (10 RP)**
The Tactical Move special combat maneuver no longer penalizes your defense, only attack.

**Wooden Familiarity (10 RP)**
Your natural comfort grants you a -1 bonus to speed with weapons that are composed of wood, or have wooden hafts. Wooden shields also have the Damage Reduction of metal shields in your hands.
