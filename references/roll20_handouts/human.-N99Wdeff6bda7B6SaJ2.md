**Adaptation (5 RP)**
You reserve up to 4 free BP per character level you have (starting with your current free BP). You may learn and unlearn proficiencies and talents at the beginning of each new day with this pool, as long as you adhere to prerequisites. Upon purchasing this talent, you may immediately move any talent already purchased with BP into this pool, as long as it does not exceed the pool's limit. Keep track of the proficiencies and talents that have been purchased with this pool.

**Destined (5 RP)**
Once per day, any effect that would kill you instead reduces you to 1 hit point away from death, and leaves you in a stable condition. Effects that kill you without reducing your hit points will still function normally.

**Determined (5 RP)**
Once per encounter, after making a failed attack or saving throw, you may roll 1d4p and add it as a bonus to potentially alter the result.

**Improvisation (5 RP)**
You are considered unskilled with even non-universal skills, and you use twice your weakest ability score to determine what you roll against for skills you are unskilled with.

**Versatility (5 RP)**
You receive no penalty for using weapons you do not have proficiency with. You must still be proficient to benefit from mastery.

**Inheritor (10 RP)**
You receive a +1 bonus to each of your saving throws.
