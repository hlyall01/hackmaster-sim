**Ironworker (5 RP)**
_requires Average Blacksmithing_
Gain a free purchase of either Armorsmithing or Weaponsmithing at each level. This is retroactive.

**Presence (5 RP)**
Your Charisma is considered 3 points higher when determining your morale modifier.

**Wetlands Immunity (5 RP)**
_requires 12+ Constitution_
You gain a +5 bonus to all communicability and severity checks against disease.

**Marshstep (10 RP)**
You can move at jogging pace instead of walking pace when using the Tactical Move, Give Ground, and Fighting Withdrawal special combat moves. Your engaged opponents may pursue if they wish, as long as their speed permits it.
