Horn of the Commander (45 BP)
requires level 8
The commander's horn is a powerful instrument, able to turn the tide of battle alone. The horn may not be used while engaged in melee, and requires a free hand. Being engaged in melee interrupts the blow of the horn - the commander now has more pressing concerns. Each of the following abilities require d3p seconds to blow the horn, and after using one the captain must take 6 seconds to recover his breath. The commander may not use either Horn of the Commander or Voice of the Commander while recovering his breath, or if he has no horn instrument.
>Rally: The commander is able to rally his fallen allies within earshot, halving the remaining duration of trauma, rounded up.
>Call to Battle: At 10th level, the commander is able to call his allies to arms. Any allies who are currently surprised and are within earshot of the horn have their Starting Initiative reduced to the current Count Up, allowing them to act immediately.
>Sound of Glory: At 12th level, the commander is able to sound his horn triumphantly, granting himself and his allies within earshot a bonus of -5 on all trauma checks for the next 60 seconds.
>Thunderous Blast: At 14th level, the commander is able to sound his horn with great force, dealing 3d4p damage in a cone 60 ft. long with a 15 ft. diameter at its base. The commander rolls a d20p and adds his level, and the targets roll their Physical Saving Throws. If their results equal or exceed the commander's, no damage is received.

Voice of the Commander (55 BP)
The commander's leadership allows him to bolster his allies and scatter his enemies in a variety of ways. The commander's voice can only take effect if its targets can hear and understand him. Each of the following abilities require d3p seconds to shout and gesture, and after using one the commander must take 6 seconds to recover his breath. Whenever a player uses Voice of the Commander, he must say an appropriate phrase for its usage. If you can’t come up with a suitable phrase, you can't use the ability, so have a few remarks prepared. The commander may not use either Voice of the Commander or Horn of the Commander while recovering his breath.
>Encourage: The commander is able to encourage an ally to strike with great precision. On their next attack roll within 6 seconds, the ally may roll twice and take the better result.
>Provoke: At 6th level, the commander is able to infuriate an enemy into stopping whatever it was doing to attack him for 6 seconds. The commander rolls a d20p and adds his level, and the target rolls its Mental Saving Throw. If the target's result equals or exceeds the commander's, it successfully resists the provocation. Once resisted, the target becomes immune to this ability until the next day.
>Commanding Shout: At 11th level, the commander is able to command an ally, who is currently between attacks, to attack. This speeds up the target's next attack by up to 4 seconds.
>Warcry: At 16th level, the commander can unleash a ferocious battle cry, immediately forcing a morale check upon enemies within earshot. If they succeed the check, they cannot be affected by this ability until the next day.
