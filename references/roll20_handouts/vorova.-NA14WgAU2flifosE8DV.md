**Bloodline (0 RP)**
_must be chosen within character creation_
At birth, you were born into a certain bloodline. Gain a benefit dependent upon your line, as follows:
• Virovoi – 1 free Survival skill purchase
• Seskovoi – 1 free Persuasion skill purchase
• Krastovoi – 1 free Intimidation skill purchase
• Askotoi – 1 free Sneaking skill purchase
• Linelost – 1 free First Aid skill purchase

**Lithe (5 RP)**
_requires female_
You gain a +1 to your dodge saving throw.

**Sturdy (5 RP)**
_requires male_
You are considered size large for knock backs.

**Heat Adaptation (5 RP)**
_mutually exclusive with frostheart_
Your body has adapted to warmer temperatures, reducing the penalty to all rolls and checks you make by 1/5% (no penalty at 86°F (30°C), and only a -1/5% penalty at 104°F (40°C) and above). In addition, you save against fire and heat-related spells and effects at -2, instead of -4, and you no longer suffer additional damage from fire and heat-related spells and effects.

**Hibernate (5 RP)
**Upon failing a trauma check you may enter a cataleptic state for one hour. While in this state, you retain auditory and olfactory senses but neither sight nor touch. As such, you are aware of your surroundings but cannot respond to physical stimulus of any type, effectively allowing you to feign death. Your metabolism is also greatly slowed while hibernating, any toxins effecting you are held in check for the duration. You may also enter hibernation or resuscitate yourself at any time, but it takes 1 minute for your body to change states.

**Endure (10 RP)**
_requires Hibernate_
Your body automatically enters a state of catalepsy when you are knocked unconscious, and it is nigh impossible to tell if you are still alive with mundane means. As your bodily systems are practically frozen in this state, you will not succumb to your wounds unless you are suffering from internal hemorrhaging or severe bleeding.

**Frostheart (10 RP)**
_mutually exclusive with heat adaptation_
Your cold-related benefits are tripled, but so are your heat-related penalties.

**Power Within (10 RP)**
The power of your blood actualizes, granting you a bonus based upon your line:
• Virovoi – +1 defense
• Seskovoi – +2 mental saving throw
• Krastovoi – +2 physical saving throw
• Askotoi – +1 damage modifier
• Linelost – resist pain (doubles your ToP target number)
