Requirements
>Alignment must be Lawful
>12+ Strength
>10+ Wisdom
>12+ Constitution
>12+ Charisma
>Lance proficiency
>Advanced Riding
>Average Persuasion
>Average Recruiting
>Average or higher Honor
>Must be knighted

Special Restrictions
>A knight may not employ ranged weapons against an honorable foe, nor polearms or other reach weapons as these may call his bravery into question. Against ignoble or lesser opponents a knight may use a bow or crossbow (as well as polearms and siege weapons) with impunity. A knight may never purposely attack an intelligent foe from the rear (vermin, beasts and the like are exempt) as this may also call his bravery into question. If a knight deigns to break these restrictions, they immediately lose a point of honor.

**Chivalry (15 BP)**
Heroic deeds are the meat and drink of knights. A knight always strives to pursue the main villain, engage the most fearsome beast on the battlefield, and so forth - and do so with conviction and in a valiant manner. A knight in Great Honor or higher receives Chivalry Points which may be spent to aid him at the key moment of a quest or mission. These points may be used to modify any of a knight’s combat-related rolls (including attack, defense, damage, saving throws, pain checks, morale, fatigue and so on), but may only be used at an epic moment of the climax of an undertaking. A knight begins each new day with an amount of Chivalry Points equal to half his level.

Devastating Charge (30 BP)
When striking a target while galloping, the knight doubles his damage dice with all weapons (including the additional dice gained from being mounted), except for lances. Instead, lances gain one additional damage dice before being doubled.

Hardened (10 BP)
Knights' Threshold of Pain is equal to 30% of their maximum hit points + another 2% per level (in place of the +1% per level less resilient characters receive). In addition, reduce your base Fatigue Factor by -2.

Inspire Others (15 BP)
A knight’s leadership ability allows him to inspire others to resist terror in the face of foes. Consequently, all allies within 10 feet of an active knight (i.e., not fallen, dead or asleep) gain a +4 bonus to all saving throws versus fear, fatigue and morale checks.

Intimidate Adversary (45 BP)
requires Inspire Others
The knight radiates an intangible aura of determination. Consequently, sentient opponents are unsettled enough to suffer a penalty of 2 to attack rolls, saving throws, fatigue checks and morale checks when within 10 feet of an active knight (i.e., not fallen, dead or asleep).

**Mounted Champion (25 BP)**
The knight defends his mount as if it were an extension of his own form. He may add half of his total defense modifier (rounded down, and excluding the bonus from being mounted) to his mount's defense.

Mounted Juggernaut (15 BP)
The knight may use two-handed weapons and fighting styles while mounted, and the knight gains an extra damage die (two for mounted weapons) against creatures of any size instead of just size M.

Mounted Mobility (20 BP)
The knight doubles the degrees he can turn while mounted, with and without ride checks. Furthermore, when performing a slide-stop maneuver, the knight may turn his mount up to 180 degrees and can enter a gallop right out of the stop instead of a canter.

Mounted Protector (10 BP)
The knight may choose to shift into the way of blows intended for his mount. When his mount is attacked, the knight may remove his mounted Defense bonus for 10 seconds, and grant +6 to his mount's Defense bonus for the same amount of time.
