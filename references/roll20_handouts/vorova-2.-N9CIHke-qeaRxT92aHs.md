Vorovoi like the cold and receive the following benefits in chilly conditions:
• a +1/5% bonus to all rolls/checks when the temperature is below 32°F (0°C)
• save at a +4 against frost and cold-related spells and effects
• receive 10% less damage from frost and cold-related spells and effects

**Cons:**
Superstitious quirk (no BP gained)
Vorovoi despise the heat and receive the following penalties in hot conditions:
• a -1/5% penalty to all rolls/checks when the temperature is above 86°F (30°C)
• a -2/10% penalty to all rolls/checks when the temperature is above 104°F (40°C)
• save at -4 against fire and heat-related spells and effects
• suffer 10% more damage from fire and heat-related spells and effects
