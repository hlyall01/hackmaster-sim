Collider
_received upon reaching Exertion Proficiency I_
With increasing experience, you are able to mitigate the effects of forceful deceleration, however your Intelligence is reduced by 1. Crashing into things is second nature to you, subtract from the distance fallen in the same manner and additive with surface conditions. This also reduces the damage taken when colliding with objects when knocked back or otherwise launched.

**Wrathful**
_received upon reaching __Exertion __Proficiency II_
You may voluntarily induce a manic state functionally equivalent to HackFrenzy. As this state is triggered at your discretion, no compulsion exists to attack a particular target. You may bypass the initial requirements for Barbarian class talents, and you count as having HackFrenzy.

**Command Beasts**
_received upon reaching __Exertion __Proficiency III_
To Command Beasts (mundane or fantastical), you must be within sight of the beast or otherwise have its attention, and command it to obey. You then roll a d20p and add both your level and your Turning Modifier (based on Charisma) to the roll. The creature rolls a d20p, adding its Mental saving throw to the result. A success of 10 or greater compels the beast into your service for 3d4p minutes. Afterwards, the term of service expires and the beast flees the area. If commanded to melee an opponent (or each other), the term of service will end immediately after the enemy is defeated and the beast will flee the area. A check that succeeds by less than 10 points results in the creature fleeing from you at its maximum rate for d4p minutes. If the results indicate a tie, the beast is held at bay for 5 seconds and then may re-engage. Command Beasts requires 5 seconds to complete, and may be performed any number of times, as long as you are successful or tie. If the beast attempts to attack you before the 5 seconds have passed, the Command Beasts attempt activates immediately. Once you fail, you may try again, but the beast receives a +4 bonus to its roll. Each successive failure results in a further cumulative +4 bonus. Attacking in melee or otherwise forcing oneself or allies onto the creature negates the repelling effects (though ranged attacks and spells do not), but does not otherwise affect future attempts or provide a bonus to the animal's check. Large groups of beasts may be commanded at once, simply roll one Command Beasts attempt and have each valid creature roll against it.

**Eyesmite**
_received upon reaching __Exertion_ _Proficiency IV_
Upon mounting a near-perfect defense, you may opt to substite Eyesmite for the customary hand-to-hand counter-attack. You must have a free hand to do so, but may opt to toss aside a secondary (or primary) weapon in order to take advantage of the opportunity presented. This permits you to attempt an instantaneous called shot to your opponent's eye. If successful, you pluck out your adversary's eyeball. A successful Eyesmite has the following consequences:
>Recipient gains the Missing Eye flaw
>Victim suffers 2d4p points of damage plus modifiers, and DR is ineffective in abrogating this damage, whether it stems from armor or is reflective of a natural carapace, inherent toughness, or size
>Victim is automatically ToP'd for 10 seconds for every point of damage inflicted
