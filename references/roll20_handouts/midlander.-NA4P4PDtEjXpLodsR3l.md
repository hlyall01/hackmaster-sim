**Common (5 RP)**
_requires Looks to between 8 and 12_
You use your Constitution to determine your Morale Modifier instead of Charisma and you gain both the Forgettable Face and No Accent talents for no LP cost. If you already had any of these talents their costs are refunded.

**Solid (5 RP)**
Your hit points are increased by 8.

**Worker (5 RP)**
Gain two free purchases of either Agriculture, Animal Herding, Animal Husbandry, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Cooking, Craft, Forestry, Leatherworking, or Pottery at each level. This is retroactive.

**Remarkability (10 RP)**
_must have at least one ability score (aside from looks) under 10_
Treat each modifier that ability scores affect (so damage, attack, initiative, defense, etc), as 1 better if the ability score gives a bonus (e.g. -1 initiative would become -2 initiative), and 1 worse if you receive a penalty from the ability score (e.g. +3 initiative would become +4 initiative). Reduce all of your ability scores by 1, aside from Looks. Finally, gain 15 additional Advancement Points.
