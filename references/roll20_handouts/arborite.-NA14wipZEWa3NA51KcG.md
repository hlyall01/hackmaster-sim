**Barkskin (5 RP)**
Your bonus to DR in natural surroundings is improved to a total of +3.

**Shillelagh (5 RP)**
You may use the mastery of your greatest melee weapon group with sticks, clubs and staves.

**Silent Defiance (5 RP)**
Whenever you are forced to perform an action due to any kind of mental effect, including but not limited to magical effects, you may choose to hold completely still instead.

**Arborhide (10 RP)**
_requires Barkskin_
Your bonus to DR no longer requires natural surroundings and is improved to a total of +8. However, you receive triple damage dice from iron objects and gain a phobia toward them (as the quirk).

**Overcome Naivety (10 RP)**
_requires an age of over five hundred years_
You gain +3 to your Wisdom score.

**Warrior Woad (10 RP)**
_requires Average Artistry (painting)_
You may apply warpaint to yourself over the course of 10 minutes. Your blood produces a strange effect when coming into contact with such art (you must receive at least 1 point of damage from a piercing or hacking instrument). You add d6 points to your strength, and gain a bonus of +6 to defense, for 5d12p seconds. Upon expiration, however, both a physical and mental saving throw must be succeeded (against d20p+11), or the sudden release of power is too much to handle, causing you to fall unconscious for about an hour.
