**Stargazer (5 RP)**
_requires Average Astrology_
You may consult the stars to guide your actions into the next day. This ability can only be used if you can observe the clear night sky. After doing so, roll 1d6. On a 1, you receive a 5% penalty to your intelligence-related skills. On a 2, nothing happens. On a 3, 4 or 5, you receive a 5% bonus to your intelligence-related skills, and on a 6 the bonus is 10%. This modifier lasts until the next night.

**Astrologue (5 RP)**
_requires Stargazer and Advanced Astrology_
You've learned to interpret the stars far better than you previously could. A 5% bonus is granted to each of Stargazer's d6 results (removing the penalty from a result of 1, and improving all other results). In addition, once per week, you can spend an hour to attempt to read the heavens for advice about a particular event or goal that will occur within 1 week. A secret Astrology check is rolled, upon a success you uncover a clue or some advice that could help with the event or goal, but on a failure this clue or advice will be misleading.

**Medicant (5 RP)**
_requires Average First Aid_
You can perform critical care at twice the speed, and it restores 1d3p points of damage from the wound instead of 1. Each individual wound may still only be treated once. In addition, while you are performing caregiver duties, you reduce the number of rest days required by an additional one per category.

**Blinding Strike (10 RP)**
_requires 13+ Dexterity_
Upon engaging in melee, you may choose to perform a contested attack roll against your opponent. The loser treats his attack roll as if it were a defense roll, and the winner lands a strike as normal. Neither of you receive another attack on the following second, regardless of weapon reach. This maneuver may not be performed if you cannot reach your opponent with your remaining movement when you engage (e.g. against spears or polearms).

**Poisonous Bite (10 RP)**
Your fangs are now poisonous, but you may still perform dry bites if you wish. The virulence factor of your poison is 9, and it can cause weakness, dizziness, headaches, breathing difficulty, nausea and death (effect 3).

**Revelator (10 RP)**
As long as you have evidence on your person that proves at least one of your conspiracy theories to be irrefutably true, you will no longer suffer migraines. If you help stop the perpetrators of your theory, you no longer need to carry around proof, can remove the Migraines flaw permanently, and gain a permanent +1 to your Charisma.
