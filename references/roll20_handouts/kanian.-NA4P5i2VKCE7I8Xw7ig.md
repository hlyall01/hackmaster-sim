**Ever Ready (5 RP)**
Readying a weapon or shield takes you at most 1 second, your caution serves you well.

**Saddleborn (5 RP)**
_requires Advanced Riding_
You are no longer thrown from your mount as a result of failed Riding checks when attempting to perform tactical mounted maneuvers.

**Beastwall (5 RP)**
You receive +1 to your defense rolls against non-human opponents.

**Isolation (10 RP)**
You hone in on isolated foes. You deal an additional 2 points of damage in melee combat when your engaged opponent has no allies within 10 feet.
