**Defiant (5 RP)**
When an enemy scores a critical hit against you, their initial damage is rolled twice and the weaker result is taken. This occurs before determining critical hit severity and additional effects.

**Gore Fiend (5 RP)**
Your antlers treat opponents as one size smaller for the purposes of knock-backs.

**Ysgrewng (5 RP)**
_requires Expert Fymblwngish_
The Ysgrewng is the signature battle roar of the Fymblwngen people. Letting loose this cry at the onset of a battle intimidates all intelligent, humanoid opponents within a 25 foot radius. Those who hear it must make an immediate morale check (excepting other Fymblwngen). Those that fail have their morale level reduced by one increment, and cowardly opponents flee on failure.

**Blood Vengeance (10 RP)**
When you suffer a hit strong enough to trigger your ToP and succeed the following Trauma save, you may immediately retaliate with your antlers even if the damage received would incapacitate or kill you.

**Impressive Rack (10 RP)**
Your antlers gain an additional dice of damage. Additionally, when charging with your antlers, you gain an additional +4 to attack and the charge damage is tripled instead of doubled for the purposes of determining knock-backs.

**Tenacious (10 RP)**
You can survive 5 more hit points into the negatives beyond what your Constitution would normally allow, and you add twice your Constitution to your stabilization checks. Additionally, you gain the Resolute talent at no BP cost. If you already had this talent, its BP cost is refunded.
