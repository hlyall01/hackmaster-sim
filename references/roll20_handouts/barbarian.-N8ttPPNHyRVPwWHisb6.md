Requirements
>Alignment cannot be Lawful
>14+ Strength
>14+ Constitution
>Average Survival
>Must purchase first barbarian talent within character creation

Special Restrictions
>Magic Phobia: Barbarians are a superstitious lot and fear magic (it being a cultural taboo). They must make a morale check at a -2 level to morale the first time they encounter any fantastic creature [i.e. one that demonstrates any power that is unnatural to include, for example, the undead, those capable of wingless flight or any being able to utilize magical powers] or magic (friendly or otherwise). Failure means they run away to safety and hide cowering. Should they witness the vulnerability of the creature, whether by viewing its remains or seeing adventuring companions live to tell the tale of the foe’s destruction, they gradually gain confidence that their mortal powers can be effective against such a supernatural foe. Encountering the specific creature a second time requires only a morale check at a -1 level while observing two instances of the beast slain by mortals removes any doubt in the barbarian’s mind that he can indeed harm the fiend with steel (and thus need not make a morale check). All barbarian are distrustful of mages even those they know well and consider non-belligerents. They will be ever wary presuming that past friendly behavior is only a ruse to gain the barbarian’s trust so as to make him more vulnerable to the nefarious manhood stealing magic that he will eventually attempt to employ. Accordingly, they will never allow a mage to cast magic upon them (even for beneficial purposes) and will seek vengeance if they become aware of surreptitious dweomers being laid upon them. If the barbarian ever becomes able to use magic he will no longer be afraid of mages and magic, but will still retain his phobia of creatures with unnatural powers.

Bridled Rage (30 BP)
requires 5th level and either HackLust or HackFrenzy
Whenever the barbarian's HackLust or HackFrenzy is triggered, he may automatically pass or fail the Wisdom check, as he chooses, to resist entering the rage. In addition, at 10th level, the barbarian may automatically pass or fail the Wisdom check to end his rage. At 13th level, the barbarian will never attack his allies if no foes remain, instead he will furiously sprint around in search of new foes.

Controlled Rampage (20 BP)
requires 8th level and either HackLust or HackFrenzy
The barbarian no longer needs to hack at his opponent's corpse while raging, and may move on to his next opponent immediately after downing someone. At 17th level, opponents can longer near-perfect defense twice as often when the barbarian is HackFrenzied.

Hardened (10 BP)
The barbarian's Threshold of Pain is equal to 30% of their maximum hit points + another 2% per level (in place of the +1% per level less resilient characters receive). In addition, reduce your base Fatigue Factor by -2.

Hearty (15 BP)
Barbarians with this talent gain 5 additional hit points. Further, they receive a +1 saving throw bonus versus poison and magic for every 3 full points of Constituion.

Natural Strength (15 BP)
When a barbarian battles natural animals (such as bears, great cats or wolves [including giant versions] but excluding fantastic creatures such as Dragons) while wearing light or no armor, he may deduct one point of Damage Reduction from the animal for each experience level he possesses. The barbarian also receives a +2 bonus to damage when engaging in unarmed combat regardless of their foe and his Strength is also considered two points higher when calculating jumping distance.

Supreme Cleave (10 BP)
requires 12th level and Cleave
The barbarian may use the Tactical Move maneuver without foregoing any extra cleave attacks.

Supreme Power Attack (30 BP)
requires 15th level and Power Attack
The barbarian may triple his Strength damage modifier while power attacking instead of doubling it.
