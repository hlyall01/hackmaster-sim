Requirements
>14+ Dexterity
>12+ Intelligence
>Average Hiding
>Average Sneaking
>Proficient with at least one Size S small sword

Assassination (35 BP)
requires level 6
The rogue deducts one from a nearhuman biped (of any size) opponent’s Threshold of Pain (ToP) for every two levels he has (rounded up). Further, for damage results of 5 or more points higher than required for a ToP check, the victim failing their Trauma check not only falls in agony but is also silenced based on the rogue’s artful choice of location (solar plexus, throat, etc.). A result of 10 or more beyond the required threshold renders the opponent completely unconscious for d6p minutes while a result of 15 or more immediately reduces the opponent to zero hit points and initiates the bleeding out rules. This talent can be used with ranged weapons, but only when within 20 feet of an opponent.

Backstab (15 BP)
When striking an opponent unaware of his presence in melee, the rogue may use a size S small sword to stab his foe in the back. This attack inflicts penetration damage on both the maximum die roll and said roll -1 (e.g., a result of 3 or 4 on a d4). Upon reaching 9th level, the rogue inflicts an additional d4p damage with a backstab and at 17th level an additional 2d4p. This supplemental damage die or dice also penetrate on a 3 or 4.

**Coup de Grâce (12 BP)**
Rogues may dispatch incapacitated opponents in 3 seconds. Most other characters require 10 seconds to execute this grisly task.

Deadly Poison (15 BP)
requires Advanced Botany and Advanced Craft (Poison)
Poisons that the rogue creates have an increased Virulence Factor of +1. Upon reaching 13th level, this improves to +2.

Devious (5 BP)
requires evil alignment
The rogue no longer suffers honor hits when poisoning sentient creatures.

Fleeing Opponent (15 BP)
requires Backstab
Should an opponent attempt to flee from melee with the rogue, the rogue can strike as if dealing a backstab. The rogue can use any weapon for this backstab. Note that the supplemental d4p or 2d4p damage gained by more experienced characters when performing a true backstab does not apply in this circumstance.

Knowledge (12 LP)
Rogues tend to learn a little bit of everything. Because of this, rogues sometimes know useless bits of trivia and other background history on just about any given topic (to wit, a 3% chance per level). The facts are only as correct or as complete as the GM’s secret percentile die rolls allow.

Hide in Shadows (25 BP)
The rogue receives a +50 bonus to hiding and sneaking skill checks as long as he is in dim light or darker.

Rapid Intoxication (30 BP)
requires level 6, Advanced Botany and Advanced Craft (Poison)
Poisons that the rogue creates begin to take effect after a 60 second delay. Upon reaching 9th level, this is further reduced to 30 seconds, and at 12th level the delay is reduced to a mere 10 seconds.

Rearward Strike (20 BP)
When the rogue strikes an opponent from behind when a melee weapon with a length of 3 feet or less, he subtracts 1 DR for every two full experience levels he possesses.

Sudden Degeneration (30 BP)
requires level 10, Expert Botany and Expert Craft (Poison)
Poisons that the rogue creates have their progression time reduced from 10 seconds to 5.

Superior Defense (15 BP)
Rogues gain a near-perfect defense on a natural roll of 18 or 19 that exceeds their opponent’s attack roll (unlike other characters who require a 19). In addition, a successful counter-blow allows the rogue to roll 2d4p for damage (instead of 2(d4p-2)).
