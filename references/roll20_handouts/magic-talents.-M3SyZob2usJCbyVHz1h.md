**Charm Resistant (16 BP)**
This talent provides exceptional resistance to magical charms (+12 bonus to saving throws).

**Combat Casting (30 BP)**
A magic user with this talent can focus enough to defend himself fully while casting a spell, instead of being limited to a d8p. In addition, your Spell Volatility penalty is treated as one category weaker when being struck, and spell failure from encumbrance or equipment is reduced by 25%. Finally, he receives a lighter penalty to his Volatility Save from being injured while casting amplified spells.

**Diminish Spell Fatigue (10 BP)**
A magic user with this talent recovers from Spell Fatigue one second sooner than normal. This talent may be purchased up to five times.

**Mitigate Spell Fatigue (15 BP)**
This talent allows a caster to better defend himself while under the effects of Spell Fatigue; the magic user in question suffers only a -3 penalty rather than a -6 penalty to defense while suffering from Spell Fatigue. He is also capable of moving at full speed, although running and sprinting are still not possible.

**Decimate Spell Fatigue (30 BP)**
_requires Mitigate Spell Fatigue_
This talent allows a caster to shrug off the effects of Spell Fatigue nearly entirely. His penalty to defense from Spell Fatigue is reduced to 0, he suffers no penalty to skill checks and all other actions require a normal amount of time to complete. In addition, instead of your weapon being considered freshly drawn upon Spell Fatigue ending, it is considered freshly drawn the moment the spell is finished casting (potentially allowing you to attack if you are particularly fast). He is also capable of moving at full speed with all modes of movement.

**Eliminate Spell Fatigue (25 BP)**
_requires Decimate Spell Fatigue, Diminish Spell Fatigue (5 ranks) and Mitigate Spell Fatigue_
This talent removes the Spell Fatigue added by a spell's casting time, effectively eliminating Spell Fatigue from the caster completely. This allows the magic user to cast back-to-back spells, although your weapon speed is still reset upon finishing a cast.

**Illusion Resistant (10 BP)**
The character with this talent will gain a +6 bonus on any Saving Throws versus illusions.

Magic Focus (15 BP)
This talent allows the character to add one sixth (rounded down) of the ability score associated with the type of spell being cast to your Magic Save.

Magic Specialist (20 BP)
requires Magic Focus
This talent improves the bonus from Magic Focus to one fifth of the associated ability score (rounded down).

Magic Master (25 BP)
requires Magic Specialist
This talent improves the bonus from Magic Specialist to one quarter of the associated ability score (rounded down).

**Silent Casting (20 BP)**
_requires level 6_
All spells can be cast without their verbal component.

**Still Casting (30 BP)**
_requires level 11_
All spells can be cast without their somatic component.

**Sleep Resistant (12 BP)**
Individuals with this talent gain a +12 bonus on any Saving Throws versus spells that induce drowsiness or sleep.
