**Contender (5 RP)**
You gain the Deceptive Defender and Precision Combatant talents at no cost. If you already had any of these talents, their BP costs are refunded.

**Duelist (5 RP)**
_requires Contender_
You no longer suffer any penalty to defense while attempting a called shot. Additionally, you gain the Combat Expertise talent at no BP cost. If you already had this talent, its BP cost is refunded.

**Haggler (5 RP)**
_requires 13+ Charisma_
You gain a free purchase of Salesmanship at each level. This is retroactive.

**Deliberate (10 RP)**
You no longer fumble your attack rolls on natural 1s, they are merely misses instead.
