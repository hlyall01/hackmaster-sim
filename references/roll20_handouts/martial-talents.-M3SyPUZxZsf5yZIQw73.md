**Advanced Sighting (30 BP)**
Through a combination of keen eyesight and practical
knowledge of trajectories, this character can mitigate the
disparaging effects of distance on Accuracy with ranged
weapons. All targets are treated as if only two-thirds
their effective (i.e. size modified) distance. Thus, if shooting at a man 60 feet away, the individual with this talent
would use 40 feet as the target distance on the ranged attack charts.

**Armor Focus (15 BP)**
_requires Light Armor Proficiency_
This talent negates the penalty to initative from wearing armor.

**Armor Specialization (20 BP)**
_requires Armor Focus_
This talent negates the penalty to speed from wearing armor.

**Armor Master (25 BP)**
_requires Armor Specialization_
This talent increases the damage reduction worn armor grants you by 1.

**Attack Bonus (specify weapon) (10 BP)**
This talent provides a +1 attack bonus with any one
melee weapon of the player’s choice. This talent may be
taken multiple times but only once for a particular
weapon.

**Blind-Fighting (30 BP)**
A character with a talent for blind-fighting is capable
of fighting in conditions of poor or no light (but this talent does not allow spell use). In total darkness, the
character suffers only a -4 penalty to his attack roll
(compared to a -8 penalty without this skill). Under
conditions of extremely dim illumination, the character
incurs only a -2 penalty. The character suffers no penalties to his Defense because of darkness or inability to
see.
This talent is effective only against opponents or
threats within melee distance of the character. Blindfighting does not grant any special protection from missile fire or anything outside the immediate range of the
character’s melee weapon.
While moving in darkness, the character may safely
proceed at a walking pace much as with blind characters.
Furthermore, this talent aids the character when dealing with invisible creatures, reducing the attack penalty
to -4. However, it does not enable the character to discover invisible creatures; he has only a general idea of
their location and cannot target them exactly.

**Blind-Shooting (40 BP)**
With this talent, a character can fire at opponents he
cannot actually see. These may be targets beyond his effective range of vision (such as goblins dimly backlit by
a torch beyond the PC’s torch or lamp light) or merely
voices and/or noises in the dark.
Shots made at dimly lit targets suffer -2 accuracy
while completely blind shots are made at -6. (The target may employ a shield for cover if held at the ready).
The GM should secretly determine if the shot hits by
comparing the archer’s attack roll versus the true distance to the target (known only to the GM) and applying cover if applicable.

**Cleave (30 BP)**
_requires Strength 12+_
If you deal a creature enough damage to make it drop (typically by killing it, knocking it unconscious or triggering its threshold of pain), you get an immediate, extra melee attack against another creature within reach. If you choose to take the extra attack, you must do it immediately without taking any other actions beforehand. The extra attack is with the same weapon and at the same bonus as the attack that dropped the previous creature. You can use this talent once per second.

**Combat Expertise (25 BP)**
Improves the Fight Defensively special combat move, halving the attack penalty from -2/4/6/8 to -1/2/3/4.

**Combat Reflexes (15 BP)**
This talent allows you to consistently attack an opponent who performs the Flee special combat move. You may still attack if you are not due to do so, even if you are also engaged by one or more of their allies.

**Crack Shot (specify weapon) (10 BP)**
A crack shot has a good eye and intuitive feel for precisely targeting opponents with ranged weapons (such
as bows, crossbows, etc.) and receives +1 to attack when firing said weapon. This talent
may be taken multiple times but only once with regard
to a particular missile weapon.

**Damage Bonus (specify weapon) (10 BP)**
Characters with this talent get a +1 bonus to damage
with one melee weapon of the player’s choice. This talent may be taken multiple times but only once for a
particular weapon.

**Deceptive Defender (17 BP)**
_requires Average Mastery in Acting_
This combat style instructs pupils in the art of misdirection. Those proficient in these techniques are able
to feign weakness in their defenses or otherwise delude
an attacker into expending effort in fruitless and easily
parried blows. This provides a +1 Defense bonus versus
an opponent’s initial attack. Subsequent attacks by the
same opponent do not merit this defensive bonus.
Should an opponent declare a Called Shot, they will
notice the change in tactics and can compensate accordingly. Proactively, they increase their opponent’s
delay to 4d4p seconds. When the blow arrives, they receive an additional +4 bonus to defense versus the called
shot.

**Dodge (25 BP)**
Gain +1 to your Defense roll. Additionally, possessing this talent
permits the defender to add the Defense modifier provided by his Dexterity (assuming he can see the approaching missile; e.g., someone isn’t shooting at his
back). Note that if a defender with the dodge talent and
a shield opts to use the latter to deflect missiles, he has
chosen not to employ his dodge talent and thus any
benefits do not apply.

**Double Weapon Focus (15 BP)**
_requires 14+ Dexterity_
This talent reduces the unmodified base speed of double weapons by 1. Additionally, with the appropriate double weapon, you may use half of your weapon mastery with the equivalent weapon group (if it is higher) instead of your double weapon mastery, as follows: axes for double axes, large swords for double scimitars, axes for dual scythes, axes or blunt for hooked hammers, basic for monk's spades, axes or spears for spear-axes, and large swords for two-bladed swords. Furthermore, you gain +4d6p additional weapon experience each weapon experience roll with double weapons if your mastery with double weapons is below its equivalent weapon group's mastery, as above.

**Double Weapon Specialization (10 BP)**
_requires level 3, 16+ Dexterity and Double Weapon Focus_
This talent allows the character count double weapons as normal weapons in regards to weapon styles as follows; battle axes for double axes, scimitars for double scimitars, scythes for dual scythes, military picks for hooked hammers, staves for monk's spades, both spears and battle axes for spear-axes, and longswords for two-bladed swords.

**Double Weapon Master (35 BP)**
_requires level 6, 16+ Strength and Double Weapon Specialization_
This talent allows the character to consider double weapons size M instead of size L for the purposes of handedness and minimum weapon speed. This also allows the use of the aggressive two-weapon fighting style while using a shield.

**Greased Lightning (specify weapon) (10 BP)**
This character is unnaturally quick at reloading missile weapons and bringing them to bear for a subsequent shot. This
talent gives him a -1 Speed bonus for ranged weapons
(subject to the weapon’s minimum speed). This talent
may be taken multiple times but only once with regard
to a particular missile weapon.

**Great Cleave (10 BP)**
_requires Cleave_
This talent removes the once per second restriction from the Cleave talent, allowing you to cleave multiple enemies if you continue to drop them within one second.

**Heavy Armor Optimization (25 BP)**
_requires Strength 15+ and Heavy Armor Proficiency_
While wearing heavy armor, add one quarter of your armor's damage reduction (rounded down) to your melee damage modifier. In addition, if you are critical hit by a hacking or piercing weapon, ignore all ancillary critical hit effects.

**Improved Critical (specify weapon group) (25 BP)**
You can now inflict critical hits on a natural 19 in addition to a natural 20, but you do not automatically hit on a 19 as you do on a 20. Your modified total attack must still exceed their modified total defense.

**Improved Reach (specify weapon group) (15 BP)**
_requires Dexterity 12+_
This aggressive combat maneuver involves a fair degree of footplay and is ill-advised for the clumsy or daft
afoot. Once mastered, it permits one to increase the effective range of lethality of hand held weaponry by an
additional foot. If, when coupled with the weapon’s natural reach, this new distance exceeds that of one’s opponent, those possessing this talent may strike the first
blow. Should the result be a tie (such as if a thief armed
with a short sword and possessing this talent meets up
with a scimitar wielding orc), the inherently longer
weapon trumps the advantage this talent provides.
This talent is only applicable to one type of weapon group though may be taken again with a different group.

**Light Armor Optimization (25 BP)**
_requires Dexterity 15+ and Light Armor Proficiency_
While wearing light armor, add half of your armor's damage reduction (rounded down) to your defense. In addition, halve the amount of extra damage taken from critical hits.

**Medium Armor Optimization (25 BP)**
_requires Strength 13+, Dexterity 13+ and Medium Armor Proficiency_
While wearing medium armor, increase your armor's damage reduction by 1 and reduce its penalty to defense by 1. In addition, reduce the severity of received critical hits by 10.

**Mobility (20 BP)**
_requires Dodge_
The Give Ground and Scamper Back special combat moves may still be used against opponents whose superior speed would normally prevent this type of maneuver.

**One-Upsmanship (10 BP)**
In ordinary circumstances, a tie between an Attacker
and Defender results in a missed attack (or a shield hit
as the case may be). However, there are certain intrepid
characters possessing both honor as well as a bit of flair
that can circumvent these restrictions that frustrate
those of lesser aplomb.
This talent, one that may only be employed in melee
combat, has a number of preconditions. First of all, it
may only be utilized by an individual possessing at least
Average Honor (note that those of lesser repute may
purchase the talent – perhaps in anticipation of someday bettering their reputation!) Secondly, one’s foe must
be less honorable. This has less to do with honor points
than honor category – thus a novice warrior in great
honor may employ this maneuver against a more experienced foe that while possessing more honor points is
in a lower category. (Should both combatants be in the
same honor category, gross point totals dictate the individual of greater honor). Last, but certainly not least, execution of One-Upsmanship must be accompanied
by a catchy, snarky or otherwise distinctive catch phrase.
Should all these conditions be met, the Attacker “one
ups” his opponent gaining a situational +1 Attack bonus
permitting him to land a blow where those of middling
stature could not.

**Parry Bonus (specify weapon) (10 BP)**
A character with this talent gets a +1 defense bonus
with any one melee weapon of the player’s choice. This
talent may be taken multiple times but only once for a
particular weapon.

**Power Attack (30 BP)**
requires 13+ Strength
When attacking with a melee weapon, you may choose to ignore any beneficial attack modifiers from your Intelligence and Dexterity and in exchange double your damage modifier from your Strength. This talent may not be used with a weapon smaller than your size (i.e. a size M character cannot benefit from this talent with a size S weapon).

**Precision Aiming (18 BP)**
Through exacting training in accuracy with ranged
weapons, this style teaches combatants to tighten their
shot grouping. When simply aiming normally (i.e. center of mass) this provides no statistically significant
bonus. However, if making a called shot, it shortens the
delay time to 1-2 seconds and reduces by half the passive defense bonus ordinarily given to targets of such
an attack.

**Precision Combatant (12 BP)**
A melee warrior possessing this talent has been rigorously trained to deliver blows to specific anatomical
locations. As such, they are exceptionally skilled at delivering called shots. When declaring one’s intent to
target a specific location on an opponent, they incur a
d4p second delay during which time they receive only
a -2 penalty to defense.
An adversary is hard pressed to defend against these
determined attacks as they are guised in skillful feints
and deceptions meant to deceive him as to the true location the attacks wishes to strike. As such, he is
granted only half the standard defense bonus normally
awarded when a called shot is declared.

**Rapid Reload (20 BP)**
This talent grants a -3 Speed bonus while using crossbows. Additionally, speed reductions are twice as effective for arbalests, and thrice as effective for heavy crossbows.

**Shield Focus (15 BP)**
This talent grants a +1 defense bonus while using shields.

**Shield Specialization (15 BP)**
_requires level 3 and Shield Focus_
A character with this talent reduces the cover value of their shield by 5, removing the need to actively crouch behind it for full projectile protection.

**Shield Master (15 BP)**
_requires level 6 and Shield Specialization_
This talent improves the Arc of Defense of shields wielded by this character as follows; bucklers improve to two frontal attackers, small shields improve to all frontal and flank attackers, and medium, large shields and tower shields improve to all frontal, flank and rear attackers.

**Spring Attack (20 BP)**
_requires Mobility_
The Tactical Move special combat move no longer penalizes the character's attack, only defense.

**Swift (specify weapon or shield) (10 BP)**
A character with this talent gets a -1 Speed bonus
with any one shield or melee weapon of the player’s choice. This
talent may be taken multiple times but only once for a
particular implement.

**Two-Weapon Fighting (20 BP)**
_requires Dexterity 12+_
When using two one-handed weapons offensively, you no longer suffers a -2 penalty to damage with the secondary weapon.

**Improved Two-Weapon Fighting (15 BP)**
_requires level 3, Dexterity 13+ and Two-Weapon Fighting or Ambidextrous_
When using two one-handed weapons offensively, subsequent attacks with the primary weapon are made on its weapon speed plus one.

**Greater Two-Weapon Fighting (15 BP)**
_requires level 6, Dexterity 14+ and Improved Two-Weapon Fighting_
When using two one-handed weapons offensively, subsequent attacks with the secondary weapon are made after a delay of its weapon speed plus one.

**Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting (35 BP)**
_requires level 9, Dexterity 15+ and Greater Two-Weapon Fighting_
This character may combine the best of both two one-handed weapon fighting styles, using the attack frequency of attacking with both weapons, and the superior defense of defending with both weapons.

Weapon Focus (specify weapon group) (10 BP)
Your base weapon experience threshold is decreased by 20% with the chosen group of weapons.

**Whirlwind Attack (25 BP)**
_requires level 6, Combat Expertise and Spring Attack_
Gain access to the Whirlwind Attack special combat move, allowing you to strike nearby creatures with a spinning attack, as follows: The attacker rotates his body and swings his weapon in a wide arc simultaneously, attacking everyone within reach with a single attack roll, at the cost of increasing weapon speed by 5 seconds after all other potential speed modifiers. A successful Whirlwind Attack results in only half damage if the defenders utilize either of the Give Ground or Scamper Back maneuvers. Whirlwind Attack may not be combined with any other special combat moves, nor can such be employed until the attacker's next attack opportunity.
